# W18a Money Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Execute this plan task by task in the assigned worktree. Claude owns integration; this branch ends at a draft PR against `codex/phase2-integration`.

**Goal:** Implement scoped invoice, payment evidence, adjustment, and follow-up records across the relay, SDK, CLI, and the r19 desktop money routes.

**Architecture:** Keep money records as Nostr events in the existing private client channel. Member commands are validated by the business-record broker, which atomically stores immutable events and relay-signed invoice heads under exact-head locks. Desktop totals derive only from authorized records already visible in each client scope.

**Tech Stack:** Rust, Nostr event builders, clap CLI, React, TanStack Query, Playwright mock bridge.

---

## Scope and invariants

- Follow the frozen r19 reference and use Manrope. Do not edit the handoff package.
- Amounts use integer minor units. Invoice currency and line totals are validated without floating point.
- Money mutations require community owner or admin authority and membership in the relevant private client channel. Other members remain read-only and limited to channels they can read.
- No collection, refund, invoice email, reminder send, tax logic, cost import, reconciliation matching, cost ledger, or profitability estimate is implemented.
- A void after any collected payment is rejected. Follow-up approval records intent only.
- Keep all added text free of em dash characters.

## File map

- `crates/buzz-core/src/business_records.rs`: schemas, command parsing, client d-tag binding, amount and currency contract helpers.
- `crates/buzz-core/src/kind.rs`: route invoice, payment, adjustment, and follow-up commands to the business broker.
- `crates/buzz-relay/src/handlers/business_records.rs`: validate authority, client scope, lifecycle, exact heads, and atomic writes; add broker regression tests.
- `docs/business-records.md`: correct and complete invoice, payment, adjustment, and follow-up field and transition contracts.
- `crates/buzz-sdk/src/business_records.rs`: typed event builders for invoice, payment, adjustment, and follow-up commands.
- `crates/buzz-cli/src/lib.rs`, `crates/buzz-cli/src/commands/mod.rs`, `crates/buzz-cli/src/commands/money.rs`, and `crates/buzz-cli/src/client.rs`: `buzz money` commands and focused CLI tests.
- `desktop/src/features/money/`: scoped record queries, mutations, derived totals, route screens, and unit tests.
- `desktop/src/app/router.tsx` and `desktop/src/features/sidebar/ui/AppSidebarBusinessSection.tsx`: register money routes and expose the existing Money destination with a minimal sidebar change.
- `desktop/src/testing/e2eReferenceWorkspace.ts`, `desktop/src/testing/e2eBridge.ts`, `desktop/tests/visual/reference-workspace-manifest.json`, and `desktop/tests/e2e/w18-money.spec.ts`: extend the single reference fixture and cover money routes and their record-backed behavior.
