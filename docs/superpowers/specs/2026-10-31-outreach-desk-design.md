## Why
The owner runs WhatsApp outreach for Horizon Labs from #Outreach. Today it is one long feed of system cards. With 100 chats he cannot see what needs him, which chat is which business, when he last messaged them, or how far each business has come (contacted, wants a call, call booked, quote, client). Success: he opens #Outreach and knows in five seconds what to do next, and every business shows its name, logo, stage and last contact.

## Goals
1. #Outreach gets outreach tabs before the normal channel tabs: "Needs you" (default, count badge), "Pipeline", "Chats" (count of chats needing a reply), then Discussion, Knowledge, Canvas, Files.
2. Needs you lists only what waits on the owner: pending batches (Approve and send N / Review each message / Reject, with business chips, the message as one business sees it, quick replies, daily cap line), unanswered replies (what they said, Scout's drafted reply if any with Send Scout's reply / Edit / Open chat), and businesses in "Wants a call" (Call <person> / Mark call booked / Open chat). Decided items leave. Empty state "Nothing needs you".
3. Pipeline: one card per business in columns Contacted, Replied, Wants a call, Call booked, Quote sent, Client; collapsed piles No thanks, Stopped, Not on WhatsApp. Card: logo, business name, person, last contacted, next step. "Find a business" search. Owner, admins and Scout can move a card (drag, and a keyboard/menu "Move to" path).
4. Chats: the existing WhatsApp inbox inside the tab, rows lead with business name, then person and stage chip, then last message preview and time. Filters All, Needs reply, Wants a call, Opted out. Selected chat shows the conversation plus a right record panel: stage with Change, 6-step track, last contacted, last reply, found by (Scout in Discovery, date, search), contact, website, Open record (only when a co-founder customer record exists), Rename contact.
5. Discussion: normal channel conversation; WhatsApp system notices (number connected, batch approved/sent/rejected, go out now) fold into one compact activity line per day that expands.
6. Logos: Logo.dev through the existing helper desktop/src/features/discovery/businessLogo.ts (businessLogoUrl(website, __COLONY_LOGO_DEV_TOKEN__), build secret COLONY_LOGO_DEV_TOKEN is set). Fallback: initials tile. Unlinked contact: person glyph and the number once, formatted (+27 68 373 5905), chip "Not linked to a business".

## Non-goals
WhatsApp profile photos (the Cloud API does not provide them). New outreach sending rules, caps or templates. Calendar, quotes or e-sign integrations. Changing the WhatsApp work area dock that opens from other channels (it stays). Co-founder mode gating: the desk works whether co-founder mode is on or off.
Anti-goals: anything that sends a WhatsApp message without the owner's approval; a pipeline that silently disagrees with the WhatsApp facts; a second stage system that drifts from the co-founder customer record.

## Stage model (relay is the source of truth)
Each WhatsApp contact has one `stage`, computed by the relay and returned in the contacts and thread views:
1. `stopped` if opted out (existing opted_out_at).
2. `not_on_whatsapp` if the latest outbound send failed with a Meta "not a WhatsApp user / undeliverable" error (builder: confirm the exact Cloud API error codes from Meta docs and the codes already stored in whatsapp_messages.error_code; record them in the relay, do not guess).
3. A manual stage set by owner, admin or Scout, if newer than the latest automatic signal (manual wins until a newer WhatsApp event arrives that implies a later stage; it never moves a card backwards automatically).
4. Otherwise derived, latest-first: `client` (linked lead converted, or co-founder customer record stage client), `quote_sent` (customer record QuoteDrafted or QuoteAccepted timeline entry), `call_booked` (customer record CallBooked milestone), `no_thanks` (inbound quick reply "No thanks"), `wants_call` (inbound quick reply "Yes, call me"), `replied` (any other inbound), `contacted` (any outbound sent), else `none` (not in the pipeline).
The relay also returns `stageSetBy` (auto or the pubkey that set it), `stageChangedAt`, `lastContactedAt` (last outbound sent), `lastReplyAt` (last inbound).
Setting a stage and renaming a contact are new owner/admin/Scout actions on the existing WhatsApp command path (prefer a signed event kind per AGENTS.md; reuse the publishWhatsAppCommand pattern the desktop already uses for batch decisions and send now). Plain members are refused. Every manual change is audited.
Rename: an owner label stored on the contact, shown everywhere instead of the WhatsApp profile name; the WhatsApp profile name stays as secondary text. Clearing the label falls back to the profile name, then the formatted number.

## Business link
A contact's `leadId` already links it to the Discovery lead that Scout found. The desktop resolves business name, place, website and person from the existing lead/prospect read models (builder: find them under desktop/src/features/discovery and sales; do not add a second lead store). If the relay can include `businessName` and `website` cheaply in the contact view, do it so the list renders without N lead lookups; otherwise resolve client side with one batched query. Unlinked contacts show the number.

## Needs you
Computed in the desktop from data that already exists: pending batches (existing pending batch query), contacts with `unanswered` true (existing flag) plus any Scout draft row for that contact (existing draft state), and contacts at stage `wants_call` not yet `call_booked`. The tab badge is the count. "Mark call booked" sets stage `call_booked`. "Call <person>" opens a tel: link.

## Constraints
Owner, admins, their agents and Outreach members read; only owner, admin and Scout write stages and labels (same access as today, plain members refused with a plain message). Works on 3.0.x clients: new contact view fields are additive; older apps ignore them. Light and dark, 1440 and 390, keyboard and pointer reach every action, one owner per label. No em dashes in any copy.

## Left to the builders
Exact event kind numbers and payload shapes, table columns, query batching, component structure, drag library choice (or none), animation. Calls made by me (coordinator), not the owner: Needs you is the default tab; the desk is not gated by co-founder mode; manual stage wins until a newer implying WhatsApp event; Chats tab embeds the existing inbox rather than a new one.

## Decisions after the builder check (coordinator calls, 31 Oct; the owner may override)
1. No thanks vs Stopped: both already opt the contact out (policy.rs is_opt_out_button / is_stop_message); that sending rule does not change. The stage reads whatsapp_contacts.opt_out_reason: a "No thanks" button tap gives `no_thanks`, a "Stop messages" tap or STOP text gives `stopped`. If the reason is not stored distinctly today, the relay starts storing it (additive) and old rows read as `stopped`.
2. Wants a call: an inbound quick-reply tap on the template's FIRST quick-reply button (the positive one: "Yes, call me", "Yes, tell me more") gives `wants_call`. Determine "first button" from the stored template components, not from literal text; fall back to the literal texts "Yes, call me" and "Yes, tell me more" only when the template is unknown.
3. Customer record link: when co-founder mode is on and a customer record exists, the relay reads its stored head (stage client) and stored timeline kinds (QuoteDrafted, QuoteAccepted, CallBooked) cheaply, never a full gather per contacts query; when off or absent, call_booked, quote_sent and client come only from manual moves (and a lead whose ProspectStage is won reads as client; lost reads as no_thanks). "Mark call booked" sets the desk stage AND, when a customer record exists and the caller may write it, appends a CallBooked milestone (kind 47069) so the record and the desk agree.
4. Next step line on a pipeline card uses real data only: contacted shows the delivery state of the last outbound ("Waiting for a reply, read"); replied shows "Reply needed" or "Scout drafted a reply"; wants_call shows "Call <person>"; call_booked, quote_sent and client show the latest customer record timeline entry when one exists. No data means no line. Nothing invented (no "Scout follows up Monday" unless such a scheduled item exists).
5. Needs you dedupe: each contact appears once, in the highest section: Wants a call, then Waiting for your reply. A Scout draft for a Wants a call contact is reachable through Open chat. The badge counts pending batches plus distinct contacts listed.
6. Discussion: pending batch cards move to Needs you and are not shown in Discussion; wa-notice rows and per-contact WhatsApp thread roots fold into one activity line per day ("3 updates: batch sent, Plane Tree replied, number connected"), expandable, each item linking to its chat or batch. Human and Scout messages render normally.
7. Missing boards: dark and 390 versions of Pipeline, Chats and Discussion are derived from the same tokens and components; at 390 Pipeline becomes a stage list (one collapsible section per stage), Chats becomes list, then conversation, then record. The PR shows them as screenshots for the owner.
8. Access: setting a stage and renaming need Access::can_send (owner, admin, owner-managed agents such as Scout, Outreach channel members), the same people who may reply on WhatsApp today. Plain members are refused with a plain message. Manual stage may be any of contacted, replied, wants_call, call_booked, quote_sent, client, no_thanks; never stopped or not_on_whatsapp (those are facts).
9. Contract: new kind 47052 KIND_WHATSAPP_CONTACT_UPDATE in the reserved block (crates/buzz-core/src/kind.rs and desktop/src/shared/constants/kinds.ts), content {"v":1,"waId":"<digits>","stage":"<stage>"} and/or {"v":1,"waId":"<digits>","label":"<text>"|null}, signed by the caller, accepted through the same path as 47049/47050. The contacts and thread views gain additive fields: stage, stageSetBy ("auto" or hex pubkey), stageChangedAt, lastContactedAt, lastReplyAt, label, profileName, businessName (when linked and cheap), website (when linked and cheap). Scout gets `colony whatsapp stage <waId> <stage>` and `colony whatsapp rename <waId> <label>` in buzz-cli.
