Colony email channel · owner brief · 7 November 2026

Email can be useful. It must not become a spam machine.

The proposal adds email to Colony's existing Outreach desk while keeping control in your hands. Nothing sends without your approval by default.

Recommended start

Connect a Google Workspace mailbox through Gmail, from a separate sending domain. Keep its login on the owner's desktop. Start with small, approved, consent-led messages. Add Microsoft 365 next. Leave generic SMTP and IMAP for later.

Biggest risk

A public email address is not automatic permission to market. South African legal advice is needed before broad cold B2B email or WhatsApp outreach.

1

Make the legal check specific

Every address gets a South African pass, hold, or block from its source, recipient, message type, consent or customer facts, and registry evidence. An unanswered check is a legal hold, not an owner override.

Cautious by default
2

Name every mailbox

Each sender has its own name, pause, daily cap, and desktop-local sign-in. The relay never holds a mailbox credential. Shared do-not-contact rules still apply everywhere.

Gmail first
3

Use a real READY verdict

Show READY only after passing SPF, DKIM, DMARC, domain-age, blocklist, bounce, unsubscribe, and sync checks. Otherwise show NOT READY with a concrete fix.

No soft pass
4

Warm slowly, then ramp

Colony does not build warm-up traffic. Hold cold mail for 21 days, then start at 3 per mailbox each day and add 2 only after a clean day, up to 25. Replies skip that cold cap, not safety stops.

No artificial warm-up
5

Stop hard on bounces

Pause all sends from a mailbox above 2 percent hard bounces in 100 campaign messages, or after 3 hard bounces in 24 hours. The owner fixes and resumes it explicitly.

Kill switch
6

Keep sequences short and local

Send at most three campaign messages: first contact, then 4 and 10 recipient-local business days later. Any reply, opt-out, bounce, complaint, or hold stops the whole business sequence.

Respect local time
7

Honor do-not-contact everywhere

Keep address and business-domain records with reasons and audited undo. Enforce them during Discovery and at send time. Every cold email includes a postal address and a plain STOP or unsubscribe line.

Company-wide
8

Detect stop requests without AI

Scan complete replies with reviewed English, Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa, and additional language packs. A bare STOP wins. A complaint or legal threat still opts out and also goes to the owner.

Permanent until undo
9

Find people carefully, skip weak fits

Use only current owners or managers on business-owned pages. Fall back to a printed general inbox only when no person is found. Require a 60-point fit score and record why every skipped business was not contacted.

No social outreach
10

Keep a complete, safe conversation

Sync to a known mailbox state before sending. Keep sent and received mail in Chats with an Awaiting reply filter. A provider 5xx keeps the draft and is never sent twice.

No duplicate send
11

Autopilot stays off

If enabled later, hold every external-domain link, Scout instruction echo, and first reply after re-consent. A Google Calendar link is allowed only in a human-reviewed reply. Scout answers honestly if asked whether it is AI.

Manual review wins
12

Protect attention and message quality

Use text-first mail with a matching minimal HTML alternative and no launch logo. Reserve 25 percent of the owner's AI plan. Treat website and reply text as data, run AI with no tools, and keep LinkedIn-style channels out of scope.
