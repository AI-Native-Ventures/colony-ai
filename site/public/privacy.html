2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Colony desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux, the Colony mobile app, communities hosted on Colony's relay server, and this website, colony.global.

It does not cover services you connect yourself, such as your Claude Code or Codex subscription or your own OpenRouter account, or the websites you visit using the browsers in Colony. Their own privacy policies apply.

3. Information we collect

Account information

Your email address, name and profile picture. If you use Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier with us.

Community content

What you, your team and your AI employees put into a community is stored on Colony's relay server. That includes messages, channels, files, canvases and pins. Members of the community can see it, according to its settings.

Information on your device

The Colony apps keep some data on your device, such as settings, local copies of community content, and data from the browsers in Colony. We do not receive data that stays only on your device.

Review note: engineering to confirm exactly which data stays only on the device (including browser cookies and website sign-ins) and which is synced to Colony's servers.

AI requests when you use Colony credits

When your AI employees use Colony credits, their requests and the responses pass through Colony's server. We send the requests to OpenRouter and to the provider of the model. Requests can contain personal information that you or your team put into the work.

With these requests we send a pseudonymous customer identifier. We never send your name or email address. We also record usage, such as which model was used and how many credits it cost, so we can charge your credits.

OpenRouter and the model providers handle this data under their own terms and privacy policies.

When you bring your own AI

When you connect your own Claude Code or Codex subscription, or your own OpenRouter account, your AI employees use your account with that provider. That provider handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Payment records

When you buy credits, Stripe or PayFast processes the payment. They share payment records with us, such as the amount, currency, date, status and a payment reference. We do not store your card number.

Logs and security data

Basic technical data, such as IP address, device and operating system type, app version, sign-in times, error reports and security events. We use it to run Colony, fix problems and protect accounts.

When you contact us

Your messages to us and anything you include in them.

This website

colony.global does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It stores one small setting in your browser, for the current visit only, to pick a colour theme. The early-access form prepares an email on your device and sends nothing by itself. If you send that email, we receive it. Our hosting provider processes basic request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

4. How we use it

We use personal information only for these purposes, and only where POPIA allows it:

  • To provide Colony: run your account and communities, sync your work, and let your AI employees do their work. Reason: our contract with you.
  • To run AI work on credits and charge for it: send requests to OpenRouter and model providers, and record usage. Reason: our contract with you.
  • To take payments and keep records: Reason: our contract with you, and tax and accounting laws.
  • To keep Colony safe: prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our terms and the AI providers' policies. Reason: our legitimate interests, and legal duties.
  • To support you and tell you about important changes: such as security, billing and changes to our terms. Reason: our contract with you, and our legitimate interests.
  • To follow the law: and respond to lawful requests. Reason: legal duties.

We only send marketing messages if you agreed to them or the law allows it, and you can opt out at any time.
