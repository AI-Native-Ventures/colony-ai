1. Two ways to pay for AI

Bring your own AI. By default, you connect your own Claude Code or Codex subscription, or your own OpenRouter account. You pay those providers directly, and their billing and refund terms apply. We cannot refund charges from those providers.

Colony credits. You can choose to buy prepaid credits from us instead. This policy covers those credits.

2. What credits are

  • Colony credits are prepaid credits that pay for the work your AI employees do inside Colony.
  • When your AI employees use credits, their requests go through Colony's server to OpenRouter and to the provider of the model they use.
  • We take usage from your credits at Colony's prices.

3. What credits are not

Credits are not money. They:

  • have no cash value, and cannot be exchanged for cash except where this policy or the law requires a refund;
  • cannot be transferred to another account or person;
  • can only be used inside Colony; and
  • never give access to raw model APIs or to OpenRouter credits.

7. Refunds

Credits are not refundable, except in these cases:

  • Where the law requires a refund. This includes rights under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002, as they apply. See Cooling-off rights.
  • Duplicate or failed charges. If you were charged twice for the same purchase, or charged for credits that were never added to your account, we will refund the charge or add the missing credits.
  • Closing your account. If you close your account, we may refund unused credits. We decide this case by case.

There are no self-serve refunds in the app. To ask for a refund, contact us as described below.

8. Cooling-off rights

If you are a consumer, section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act may let you cancel a purchase of credits within 7 days, without giving a reason and without penalty. If it applies and you cancel, we will refund your payment within 30 days of the cancellation. To cancel, email [support email] within the 7 days.

The Act lists some exceptions to this right in section 42.

Review note: the owner and a lawyer must confirm how the section 44 cooling-off right applies to digital credits, including credits partly used within the 7 days (section 42(2)(d) excludes services that began with the consumer's consent before the 7 days ended), and how much of a partly used purchase is refunded.

9. How to ask for a refund

Email [support email] with:

  • the email address on your Colony account;
  • the date and amount of the payment;
  • your receipt or payment reference; and
  • why you are asking for a refund.

We will reply within [response time]. If we approve a refund, we pay it back to the payment method you used, in the currency you paid. Your bank may use its own exchange rate. We remove the matching credits from your account.

10. Chargebacks

If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us first. We can usually fix it faster than your bank. If you dispute a charge with your bank, we may pause the use of credits on your account until the dispute is settled. If the dispute succeeds, we remove the matching credits.

11. Suspended or closed accounts

While an account is suspended, its credits cannot be used. If we close an account because it broke our Terms of Service, our Acceptable Use Policy, or the terms of OpenRouter or a model provider, we do not refund unused credits, unless the law requires it.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. Changes do not take away a refund you have already asked for under an earlier version. The date at the top of this page shows when we last changed it. For important changes, we will tell you in advance, as our Terms of Service describe.
