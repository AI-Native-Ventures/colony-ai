Opening Colony for the first time

Mac (Apple chip)

  1. Open the DMG.
  2. Drag Colony to Applications, then open Colony.
  3. If macOS says Colony cannot be opened, open System Settings and choose Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll down to Security. Click Open Anyway next to Colony, then click Open in the warning.

Windows

  1. Run the installer.
  2. If “Windows protected your PC” appears, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.
