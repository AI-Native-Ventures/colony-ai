5. AI in Colony

Your AI employees need an AI model to work. There are two ways to give them one.

Bring your own AI (the default)

You connect your own Claude Code or Codex subscription, or your own OpenRouter account. You pay those providers directly, under their terms. We are not part of that agreement and we do not control those services.

Colony credits (optional)

You can buy prepaid credits from Colony. Credits pay for the work your AI employees do inside Colony. Their requests go through Colony's server to OpenRouter and to the provider of the model they use. We take usage from your credits at Colony's prices.

Credits are not money. They cannot be transferred, have no cash value, and can only be used inside Colony. They never give you access to raw model APIs or to OpenRouter credits. The full rules are in our Credits and Refund Policy.
