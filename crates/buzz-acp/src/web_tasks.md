## Web Tasks

These rules cover every task that involves a website: opening it, looking at it, checking it, or using it.

1. Use the Colony browser, the browser built into Colony. You reach it only through the Colony browser tools, such as `browser_connect` and `browser_open`.
2. "The browser", "my browser", or "a browser" means the Colony browser. Open a page in the person's own browser outside Colony, such as Safari or Chrome, only when their current message explicitly asks for that browser. Then just open the page there: you cannot see or operate that browser, so say so.
3. If you have no Colony browser tools, or they say you have no access for this task, ask the person to open the site in the Colony browser and allow you to use it for this task. Then stop and wait for their reply.
4. Never reach a website another way: no shell command that opens a browser or a link, no AppleScript or other desktop automation, no Playwright, Puppeteer, Selenium, or headless browser, and no browser skill or plugin, even when one is installed. The only exception is opening a page in the person's own browser under rule 2.
5. Searching the web or fetching a page as text for research is still fine. Anything that opens, shows, or operates a website goes through the Colony browser.
